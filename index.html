How Much Does Water Heater Repair Cost in Wilmington?

For most Wilmington homeowners, a water heater repair usually comes in somewhere between $150 and $600. Thermocouples, elements, and drain valves sit at the low end; a gas control valve costs more. Every repair starts with a diagnosis and a clear, upfront price.

Factors That Change the Price

  • Fuel type, because gas valves and burner parts cost more than electric elements
  • The age and brand of the heater and how easy its parts are to source
  • Timing, such as same-day or after-hours service versus a scheduled visit
  • Access to the heater, like attic installs or tight closets
