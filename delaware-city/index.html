Keeping Your Delaware City Water Heater Running Longer

The best way to avoid a surprise leak is simple maintenance. We recommend that homeowners flush the tank once a year (more often with hard water), have the anode rod checked every three to five years, lift the T&P valve lever annually to make sure it isn't stuck, and keep the thermostat around 120 degrees, which limits scalding risk and cuts down on scale. Tankless owners should plan on descaling once a year if their water is hard.

Equipment age plays a big role. With regular service, a standard tank typically lasts 8 to 12 years, a heat pump water heater 10 to 15, and a tankless unit up to 20. Once a heater passes that range and starts showing warning signs, like discolored hot water, popping noises, or moisture under the tank, it's smart to start planning a replacement. A planned replacement is cheaper and is far less stressful than an emergency swap after a leak.

Minor issues don't stay minor for long. Take a spent anode rod: swapping it out is a quick, inexpensive repair, but once it's gone, the water begins corroding the tank steel, and just like that a minor fix becomes a full replacement. That's why we ask homeowners to keep our number in their phone and call at the first sign of trouble. Catching it early saves money and prevents a flooded utility room.

What Goes Wrong With Local Water Heaters

In Delaware City, we work on every kind of water heater, and each one has a typical way of failing. Gas tank heaters tend to have trouble with pilot assemblies and thermocouples, sooty burners, and draft problems in the flue. Electric units burn out elements and thermostats, especially when sediment buries the lower element. Tankless heaters show error codes for scale in the heat exchanger, ignition faults, and flow sensor problems, most of which trace back to skipped descaling. Heat pump water heaters depend on a clean filter and a free-flowing condensate line, and they don't do well in small, closed-in spaces. Our technicians work on all four every week and carry the parts to fix most problems on the first visit.
