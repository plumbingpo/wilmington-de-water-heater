Hot Water for Kitchens, Restaurants, and Food Facilities

Dish machines, sanitizing sinks, and handwashing stations all compete for the same hot water, and a heater that's undersized or failing shows up first as a dish machine that can't hit temperature and a backed-up dish room. A high-temperature dish machine typically needs final rinse water near 180°F, which usually means a booster heater paired with the main system. We handle high-input tanks, tankless racks, booster heaters, and sizing for peak demand, and we schedule every visit around your prep and service times.

Hot water temperature is a core issue for any food-service operator. Health inspectors check water temperature at handwashing sinks and sanitizing stations, and a dish machine that can't reach its rinse temperature can stop service. Our work keeps your system delivering the temperatures and volume your operation depends on, with written service records that show a well-maintained facility to health inspectors and landlords. You get detailed reports after each visit, along with the documentation many commercial leases require.

We work directly with owners, managers, and kitchen staff to plan replacements and maintenance that protect your business and fit your operating schedule. Our technicians know how to work in occupied facilities, installing after close when needed, minimizing disruption, and keeping part of the system online whenever the layout allows.

Safe Installation

Safe Installations, Documented Service

Safety is part of every commercial job we do. Our technicians inspect flue draft and vent connections, verify adequate combustion air, check relief valves and their discharge lines, verify expansion tanks are charged to the right pressure, and adjust mixing valves so water stored at higher temperatures leaves the tap at a safe temperature. We pull permits and schedule inspections where required. Findings go into a written report that ranks each issue by priority and recommends a specific fix.

This work serves a dual purpose: it protects people and property, and it provides the documentation that demonstrates due diligence to landlords, insurers, and inspectors. We maintain detailed service logs, temperature records, relief valve test dates, and a documentation binder for every account, with more frequent service for heavy-use equipment in kitchens, laundries, and large mechanical rooms.
